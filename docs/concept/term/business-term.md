# Business Term

Business Terms are user-facing terminology used in conversations,
documentation, UI, and policies.

## Purpose

The primary purpose of a Business Term is to provide a human-readable
label for a [Concept](../concept.md). By linking multiple Business
Terms (synonyms) to a single Concept, the Enterprise Knowledge Graph
ensures that everyone is talking about the same underlying meaning
even if they use different words.

## Examples

- “Customer”
- “Counterparty”
- “Risk Position”

## Lexical Variants

A single Business Term often has multiple forms for different
contexts:

- **Singular**: “Patient”
- **Plural**: “Patients”
- **Abbreviation**: “Pat.”
- **Short label**: “P”
