    Production-proven

    A method refined across real EKG use cases running in production.

    What is the Use Case Tree Method?¶

    The Use Case Tree Method is a business-owned approach to turn strategy into reusable EKG components — without boiling the ocean.

    Strategic capabilities like Enterprise Risk Management or Client 360 are broken down into smaller use cases that you can — and should — deliver first. Each use case is a reusable building block (see "Modularity Managed").

    The Use Case Tree in one sentence¶

    The Use Case Tree is a high-level map of the capabilities your enterprise plans to build — broken down into use cases with dependencies, so you can deliver in the right order.

    • Plan: Long-term data strategy and business capability plan.
    • Scope: High-level requirements overview and dependency model.
    • Align: One single artifact for business, data, and technology specialists.
